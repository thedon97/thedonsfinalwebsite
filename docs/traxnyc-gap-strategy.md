# TraxNYC Gap Strategy

This analysis uses public pages and public search results for legitimate competitive research. It does not copy TraxNYC text, images, code, customer data, or protected assets.

## Public Strengths Observed

- Deep collection navigation across chains, pendants, rings, earrings, bracelets, watches, gifts, gemstones, and price bands.
- A multi-step custom project form with visual jewelry, metal, stone, style, upload, timeline, budget, and contact choices.
- Dedicated engagement-ring builder, diamond search, and previous-project pathways.
- A large education/blog footprint around diamonds, chains, watches, gold authenticity, style history, and custom projects.
- Strong founder-led video, social distribution, entertainment content, press mentions, and recognizable project showcases.
- Third-party review and discussion visibility across Google, Facebook, Yelp, Trustpilot, Reddit, media, and industry relationships.

## Original Don Jewelers Response

- Lead with private engagement-ring consultation and custom flexibility instead of trying to match catalog volume.
- Publish original CAD-to-finished-ring case studies with customer permission, timelines, specifications, revisions, and final photography.
- Build focused shape/setting education that links directly to live diamonds, custom design, and appointments.
- Add a previous-project gallery only from owned or licensed work.
- Record short expert videos answering one buying question each; embed transcripts on matching website guides.
- Create service-specific outreach assets for proposal planners, wedding venues, photographers, bridal boutiques, and local publications.

## Legitimate Backlink Program

Backlinks cannot be copied or transferred. Pursue equivalent classes of earned links:

1. Complete consistent profiles on Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, MapQuest, BBB where eligible, local chambers, and wedding directories.
2. Partner with Lehigh Valley and NYC wedding photographers, planners, venues, florists, bridal boutiques, and proposal services on original guides and styled shoots.
3. Pitch original CAD/process stories and local business features to regional publications and jewelry trade media.
4. Ask suppliers and manufacturers for authorized retailer/designer directory links where the relationship is real.
5. Publish embeddable ring-size, diamond-report, shape, setting, and custom-timeline resources worth citing.
6. Connect YouTube, Instagram, Pinterest, Facebook, and GBP posts to the exact relevant website page with consistent campaign UTMs.
7. Never buy bulk links, reproduce competitor press releases, post fake reviews, or create deceptive directory profiles.
