# Content SEO Plan

## Publishing Standard

Every article must solve a real customer question, use an original slug/title/description, identify The Don Jewelers & Jewelry as author or publisher, include visible publication and updated dates, link to relevant products/services, and receive human review before publication. Drafts must be `noindex` and excluded from the sitemap.

Do not auto-publish rewritten competitor pages, mass city pages, unsupported claims, or thin AI summaries. Consolidate overlapping articles with redirects and canonicals.

## 90-Day Clusters

1. Engagement-ring decisions: setting styles, diamond shape, ring sizing, custom process, timeline, budget drivers, proposal deadlines.
2. Diamond education: lab versus natural, CVD basics, certificate reading, cut/color/clarity tradeoffs, fancy shapes.
3. Custom process: consultation, sketches, CAD, approval, casting, setting, quality review, insured delivery.
4. Jewelry buying: tennis bracelet sizing, chain width/length, pendant design, gold karats, care and cleaning.
5. Local intent: one substantial Lehigh Valley buying guide and one NYC sourcing guide, each containing genuinely different logistics and customer value.
6. Proof of craft: original CAD-to-finished-piece case studies with permission, process photos, specifications, and client-safe storytelling.
