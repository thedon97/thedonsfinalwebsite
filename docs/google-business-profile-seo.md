## Manual Dashboard Work

- Primary category: select the most specific available category that represents the core business, typically `Jeweler` or `Jewelry store`; confirm the live choices before changing because Google may require reverification.
- Secondary categories: consider `Diamond dealer`, `Jewelry designer`, and an engagement-ring-specific category only if available and genuinely accurate.
- Do not add categories for products or services not actually offered.
- Service areas must use specific cities/postal areas, up to Google's limit. Nationwide shipping belongs in website copy; it should not be represented as an unlimited local service radius.
- If customers are not served at a staffed, signed location during stated hours, hide the street address in GBP and use accurate service areas.
- Add the UTM website and appointment URLs, services, product highlights, current photos, and one useful weekly post.
- Ask real customers for reviews without incentives. Reply naturally and mention the actual service/location only when relevant to that review.
- Seed Q&A only with genuine common questions and accurate answers.
