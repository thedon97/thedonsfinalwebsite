# Google Ads And Free Listings Plan

## Free Setup Priorities

1. Google Business Profile products
Add service/product entries with high-end images and links to matching pages.

2. Google Merchant Center free listings
Use approved ready-made products and product images. Start with engagement rings, tennis bracelets, pendants, earrings, and chains.

3. Review generation
Text the Google Business Profile link to every real customer after delivery or pickup.

4. Citation listings
Keep name, phone, website, and service area consistent on Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, Facebook, Instagram, Trustpilot, The Knot, WeddingWire, Nextdoor, and local chamber sites.
